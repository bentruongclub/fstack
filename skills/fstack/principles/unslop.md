# Unslop

Cut AI tells. Leave the human voice. Fuji bar, not a style essay.

## When

Before you call prose done. Skills, README, commit messages, replies.

## What counts

- Plain words. Short sentences. Specific names and paths.
- Opinions that cost something. Cut the hedge when you know.
- Rhythm that varies. One punchy line, then a longer one if needed.

## What does not

- "Certainly", "I'd be happy to", "Great question", "I hope this helps".
- Em dashes. Cheerleading. Pep talk. Bold on every noun.
- Puffery: pivotal, delve, landscape, tapestry, underscore, leverage.
- A manifesto of banned words longer than this file.

## Do

1. Read what you wrote once for tells.
2. Cut or rewrite the tells. Keep meaning.
3. Keep impatient-editor voice from `SKILL.md`.
4. Done when a stranger would not clock it as default model paste.

One principle. Not a catalog.
